Fig. 1. Overview of the six-module analytical framework: (1) development of the 104-gene HOTAIR-interactome testbed from RNA pull-down and literature curation; (2) implementation of a dual-source S-PrediXcan TWAS; (3) assessment of cross-source agreement; (4) two-axis discordance decomposition, treated throughout as a diagnostic partition rather than a structural decomposition because the axes are not separately estimable at this sample size; (5) disease-agnostic calibration using housekeeping controls, followed by cross-cohort replication; and (6) integration into a TWAS-specific, STREGA-based reporting checklist. DR, diabetic retinopathy; DN, diabetic nephropathy; DPN, diabetic peripheral neuropathy; TWAS, transcriptome-wide association study; eQTL, expression quantitative trait locus; GTEx, Genotype-Tissue Expression Project.
