Fig. 2. Covariate balance before and after Mahalanobis matching. This panel displays standardized mean differences (SMD) for three matching covariates—log10-transformed gene length, GC content (%) and eQTL SNP count—comparing 30 candidate genes (red circles, pre-match) with their 30 Mahalanobis-matched controls (blue squares, post-match); the dashed vertical lines mark the |SMD| < 0.25 adequacy threshold. Missing eQTL SNP counts were imputed using the median in accordance with the predefined protocol (Methods). SMD, standardized mean differences.
