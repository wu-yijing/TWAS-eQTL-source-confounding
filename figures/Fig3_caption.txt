Fig. 3. Cross-source TWAS Z-score agreement (GTEx v8 Whole_Blood vs eQTLGen). (a) The S-PrediXcan Z-scores for the 96 gene–phenotype pairs (32 genes across DR, DN, and DPN); the dashed line marks y = x, with Spearman ρ, 95% CI, and direction consistency shown inset. (b) GTEx v8 Whole_Blood Z-score distributions by phenotype, with direction consistency for each complication above each boxplot. For legibility, three pairs with extreme GTEx Z-scores (RPL10A: −107.4, −45.0, and −52.7 in DR, DN, and DPN) fall outside the plotted window in both panels but are included in all reported statistics.
