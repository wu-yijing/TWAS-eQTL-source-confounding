Fig. 6. eQTL-source FDR enrichment shift across the three diabetic complications. Group-stratified FDR-significant rates (q < 0.05) are shown for each phenotype (a: diabetic retinopathy; b: diabetic nephropathy; c: diabetic peripheral neuropathy) using GTEx v8 ACAT-O (red) and eQTLGen BH-FDR (blue) weights for candidate, non-candidate and T2DM control genes. Error bars are 95% exact Clopper–Pearson confidence intervals; numbers above the bars give the ratio k/N.
