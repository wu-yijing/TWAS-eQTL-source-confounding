Fig. 7. Cross-cohort replicability of a source-stable finding (RNH1). (a) RNH1 S-PrediXcan Z-scores using GTEx v8 Nerve_Tibial and eQTLGen across DR, DN and DPN. (b) Random-effects meta-analysis of RNH1 in DR using eQTLGen weights (FinnGen R13 Z = +13.32, N_e = 59,134; UK Biobank ieu-b-4803 Z = +0.95, N_e = 54,209): Q = 76.5, P = 2.2 × 10⁻¹⁸; I² = 98.7%; τ = 8.69; pooled mean Z = +7.13 (SE 6.18; test P = 0.25); 95% prediction interval [34] −13.77 to +28.04 (orange), spanning zero. A third UK Biobank dataset (GCST90043640; Z = +0.57, N_e ≈ 1,231) was underpowered and excluded from the pooled estimate. With k = 2 cohorts the heterogeneity statistics and prediction interval are descriptive only, and the qualitative between-cohort contrast is the primary evidence; the pooled Z is an unweighted mean of the two cohort Z-scores (Methods). N_e, effective sample size; Q, Cochran's heterogeneity statistic; τ, between-study standard deviation; PI, prediction interval.
