Fig. 8. Cross-trait generalization of the within-GTEx tissue-context comparison (Whole_Blood vs Nerve_Tibial) across three gene sets. The HOTAIR-testbed and housekeeping panels utilize data from the FinnGen R13 GWAS; the PGC3 SCZ panel relies on the PGC3 schizophrenia GWAS dataset [22]. Data points represent Spearman's ρ accompanied by Fisher z-transformed 95% confidence intervals, where n denotes the number of gene–phenotype pairs. The three panels rest on different gene universes and are therefore not directly comparable: the testbed estimate uses all 50 genes with valid Z-scores in both GTEx tissues (n = 150), which is the full tissue-only arm of Table 3b, the housekeeping estimate uses the 22 control genes with both-tissue Z-scores, and the PGC3 SCZ estimate is genome-wide. SCZ, schizophrenia; PGC, Psychiatric Genomics Consortium.
