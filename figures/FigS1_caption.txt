Fig. S1. Exploratory diagnostic scheme for dual-source TWAS sensitivity analysis. The flowchart guides analysts from a single-source TWAS through source comparison, matched-enrichment validation and cross-cohort replication, with direction-consistency branch points (>75%, 60–75%, <60%) keyed to cut-points derived from the HOTAIR testbed distribution and externally calibrated on the genome-wide schizophrenia benchmark (PGC3 SCZ; Methods) and reported in the main text. The framework does not require the two axes to be statistically separable; it is anchored to direction consistency alone.
