Table 1. GTEx vs eQTLGen Z-score Comparison for Core Genes.
Summary of key candidate genes showing the most extreme eQTL-source-dependent discordance across diabetic complications. Columns: Gene symbol, Complication (DR/DN/DPN), GTEx Z-score, eQTLGen Z-score, Direction Consistency (Consistent/Inconsistent), and Annotation. CKAP4 shows cross-complication signals under GTEx. Note: eQTLGen Z values updated 2026-08-11 to match candidate_comparison_DR.csv (v2.0.0 analysis); earlier v1.0.0 values (eQTLGen Z=+1.18) are superseded.
