Table 2. FDR Enrichment Rates by Group, Phenotype, and eQTL Source.
FDR significant rates (%) for three gene groups (Candidate, Non-Candidate, T2DM Control) across DR/DN/DPN under GTEx v8 and eQTLGen weights. Candidate genes showed substantial FDR enrichment under both eQTL weight sources (GTEx: 44.4–51.9%; eQTLGen: 53.6–71.4%), and disease-irrelevant control groups (non-candidate interactors and T2DM controls) attained comparable or higher enrichment rates (up to 75.9%), indicating that TWAS enrichment is substantially driven by cis-eQTL architecture rather than complication-specific biology. Note: values updated 2026-08-11 to match enrichment_comparison.csv (v2.0.0 analysis); earlier v1.0.0 values (GTEx 40.7% / eQTLGen 0%) are superseded.
